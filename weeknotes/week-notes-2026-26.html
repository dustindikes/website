📚 Read

  • One Million Passports Leaked Online I really wish this hadn't become the norm. We should not be expected to provide sensitive government-issued credentials to private companies, such as SSNs and passports. Those are government documents that should be used to identify yourself to government entities. A lot of breaches wouldn't matter so much if we didn't allow the storage of government-issued credentials.
  • I Left Port 22 Open on the Internet for 54 Days. Here's Who Showed Up. (Found in 82MHz's Linkdump No 113 ) This was a really interesting read! It's very statistics heavy, diving into what was found on their honeypot that was up for 54 days. Only a few people actually connected via ssh, which was surprising. I expected more, honestly. Overall it's very detailed and interesting to see how random, open machines are being hit constantly.
